## 1. What the membership is

ClearFlow Membership lets you use ClearFlow's AI features — judging replies and enhanced translation, including YouTube captions — through our servers, without getting your own API key. The features are the same as with your own key, except that images in replies are not checked.

- New accounts get one free 7-day trial, with no card needed: one trial per email address and per device. During the trial, the daily usage limit is lower.
